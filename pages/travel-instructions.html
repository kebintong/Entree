Travel Instructions

Everything you need to know for a smooth ferry journey

Important Reminder

Please arrive at the terminal at least 1 hour before departure. Late arrivals may result in denied boarding.

Before Your Trip

Required Documents

Bring a valid government-issued ID and your booking confirmation. Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian with proper documentation.

Baggage Guidelines

Each passenger is allowed:

  • 2 pieces of hand-carry luggage (max 7kg each)
  • 2 pieces of check-in baggage (max 20kg each)
  • Excess baggage subject to additional fees

At the Terminal

Check-in Process

  1. Proceed to the ticket counter for check-in
  2. Present your booking reference and valid ID
  3. Check in your baggage at the baggage counter
  4. Proceed to the waiting area after security check
  5. Board the vessel when your ferry is called

Terminal Facilities

Most terminals have waiting lounges, restrooms, convenience stores, and food stalls. Free WiFi is available in most major terminals.

During the Trip

Onboard Amenities

Most ferries offer:

  • Air-conditioned seating areas
  • Restrooms and washing facilities
  • Snack bars and cafeterias
  • Outdoor viewing decks
  • TV entertainment in tourist class

Safety Guidelines

  • Wear your life vest when instructed by crew
  • Follow all safety announcements and instructions
  • Keep children supervised at all times
  • Do not smoke except in designated areas
  • Report any emergencies to crew immediately
